# Resources

A curated list for people working on QUBO models and quantum circuit compilation. Suggestions welcome by pull request.

## 4colors

- [Docs](https://docs.4colors-research.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=examples&utm_campaign=resources)
- [`fourcolors` on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/fourcolors/)

## QUBO and Ising modeling

- F. Glover, G. Kochenberger, Y. Du, "A Tutorial on Formulating and Using QUBO Models", 4OR 17 (2019); arXiv:1811.11538.
- A. Lucas, "Ising formulations of many NP problems", Frontiers in Physics 2 (2014); arXiv:1302.5843.
- [dimod](https://github.com/dwavesystems/dimod): shared model types for QUBO and Ising tools in Python.

## Quantum circuit compilation

- [Qiskit transpiler documentation](https://docs.quantum.ibm.com/guides/transpile).
- [OpenQASM 3 specification](https://openqasm.com/).
